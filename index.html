About Us

Southern Gourmet Bites is a premium handcrafted chocolate brand rooted in tradition and inspired by the rich flavours of South India. Based in Mathur, we create artisanal chocolates using locally sourced ingredients, time-honoured techniques, and a passion for quality. Every product reflects our commitment to authentic taste, sustainability, and craftsmanship.

Discover Southern Gourmet Bites – A Taste of South India

Located in Mathur, Southern Gourmet Bites specializes in handcrafted chocolates made with locally-sourced ingredients and rich South Indian flavours. Our treats are pure, eco-friendly and packed with tradition. Explore our range and experience the perfect blend of taste, culture, and quality – one bite at a time.

  • Cocoa sourced from the rich soils of Andhra Pradesh
  • Direct partnerships with local farmers
  • Pure, eco-friendly and sustainably packed
